Accessibility principles

Semantic structure

Landmarks and meaningful headings help assistive technology users navigate the page.

Keyboard support

Visible focus indicators and keyboard-operable controls support users who do not use a mouse.

Clear feedback

Forms provide explicit labels, required-field information, and readable validation messages.
